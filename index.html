[ FREQUENTLY_ASKED ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in Lakshya '26.

Who is eligible to participate in Lakshya '26? +
Lakshya is open to all university students (undergraduate & postgraduate), researchers, fresh graduates, and self-taught developers worldwide. No prior hackathon experience required.
What is the team size limit? +
Teams can have between 1 to 4 members. You may register as a solo hacker or bring up to 3 teammates. Inter-college teams are fully permitted!
Is there any registration fee? +
Zero! Lakshya '26 is completely 100% free of cost. High-speed internet, meals, snacks, red bull / coffee, swag bags, and sleeping arrangements are fully sponsored.
Can I build hardware or IoT projects? +
Yes! We have a dedicated ClimateTech & Smart Hardware track. We also provide a maker lab with basic soldering stations, Arduino/ESP32 kits, and test benches.
Who owns the intellectual property (IP)? +
You do! 100% of the intellectual property, code, and inventions created by your team during the hackathon remain solely yours.

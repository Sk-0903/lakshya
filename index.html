[ FAQ_ARCHIVE ]

Hackathon FAQ

Everything you need to know about participating in Lakshya '26.

Who can participate in Lakshya? +
Lakshya is open to all university students (undergraduate, postgraduate), researchers, and early-stage professionals worldwide. No prior hackathon experience required.
What is the team size policy? +
Teams can have between 1 to 4 members. You can either register with friends from the same university or collaborate cross-institute.
Is there any registration or entry fee? +
No, absolutely zero. Registration is 100% free. All accepted teams will receive complimentary accommodation, food, energy drinks, Wi-Fi, and swag packs.
Can we build hardware prototypes? +
Yes! We have an on-site Hardware & IoT lab equipped with 3D printers, soldering stations, microcontrollers (ESP32, Arduino, Raspberry Pi), and sensors available on request.
Who owns the intellectual property (IP)? +
You do! 100% of the intellectual property, code, and inventions created by your team during the hackathon remain solely yours.
